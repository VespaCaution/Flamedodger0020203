# Flame dodger privacy policy

PRIVACY POLICYLast Updated: September 17, 2026This Privacy Policy describes how I, VespaCaution, handle data for my game, Flame Dodger, when played on the CrazyGames platform. I care about your privacy and want to be transparent about how data is used in my Game.No Contact Information CollectedI do not collect, request, or store any personal contact information. You will never be asked to provide your real name, email address, phone number, physical address, or account login credentials to play the Game.Information Collected by Third-Party ServicesTo provide core gameplay features, online rankings, and advertisements, my Game integrates trusted third-party services. These services handle data independently according to their own privacy policies:GDevelop Cloud Services (Leaderboards): The Game features a global leaderboard system powered by GDevelop. If you choose to submit your score, the username/nickname you type in and your game score will be sent to and stored on GDevelop's servers to display public rankings. You can view their data practices in the GDevelop Privacy Policy.CrazyGames: The Game uses the CrazyGames SDK to display advertisements and track platform achievements. CrazyGames may automatically collect device information, IP addresses, and cookie data to optimize performance and serve ads. You can view their practices in the CrazyGames Privacy Policy.Local Data Storage (LocalStorage)The Game uses your web browser's local storage to save your personal game progress, settings, and high scores locally. This data stays entirely on your own device. I cannot access, view, or modify this local data.Children’s PrivacyThe Game is intended for a general audience. Because I do not collect any personal contact information or track user identities, the Game does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. Players are advised not to use their real names as public leaderboard nicknames.Data DeletionIf you have submitted a nickname to the global leaderboards and wish to have your score or nickname removed from the public rankings, you can submit a removal request directly through GDevelop's standard cloud data management channels or platform support.
